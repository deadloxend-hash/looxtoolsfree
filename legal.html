Legal & Operational Information

An overview of LOOX service boundaries, processing and user responsibilities.

Operational overview

LOOX is an independent browser-first utility suite. Access to the platform is subject to the published Terms of Use, Privacy Policy, Cookie Policy and License Agreement.

Two processing paths

  • Local processing: Marked tools work inside the browser and do not send the selected file to a LOOX processing server.
  • Connected processing: Marked cloud features send selected content over an encrypted connection after the user starts the operation.

Service boundaries

Tools are provided on an “as available” basis. Output and performance depend on source files, browser capabilities, device resources and connected service availability. Nothing on this page removes rights that cannot legally be excluded in a user’s jurisdiction.
