Storage & Workspace Preferences

Review and clear locally saved LOOX interface preferences.

What is saved locally

LOOX may store your theme, language, sidebar state, favorite tools, recent tools and default workspace settings in this browser. Uploaded media, drafts and generated passwords are not saved by this preference panel.

Reset this browser

This removes LOOX interface preferences stored on this device. It does not delete hosting logs or storage controlled by another domain.
