How it works

1. Select a video. 2. Enter the start and end times in seconds. 3. Trim and download the selected section.

Privacy: Video trimming runs locally in your browser using FFmpeg WebAssembly. Your video is not uploaded to LOOX. The processing engine is downloaded from jsDelivr when needed. Large files require extra memory.

LOCAL / NETWORK

Your files deserve a clear explanation

Trimming runs locally in your browser using FFmpeg WebAssembly. Your video is not uploaded to LOOX by the trimming workflow. The engine is downloaded from a CDN, and very large videos may exceed device memory.

A thoughtful final check

Review identifying details, confidential information and the final file before sharing. Local processing does not automatically remove metadata or guarantee anonymity. AI-generated or heavily edited content may need clear labeling.

LOOX HELP

Questions, answered

Are times entered in seconds?

Yes. Enter the start and end values in seconds as shown by the tool.

Why might a trim fail?

Unsupported codecs, very large files, browser memory limits or an incomplete media-engine download can interrupt processing.

Is my video uploaded?

No. Trimming is performed locally in the browser; the media engine is fetched from a CDN.

Will this replace my original file?

The tools create a separate output. Keep the original until you have checked the exported file; the site does not provide cloud project backups.

Can I publish the result?

Use source media you own or have permission to edit. Check the destination’s requirements and the rights of anyone shown before publishing.
