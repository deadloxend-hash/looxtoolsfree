Write better prompts for AI images

Start with a clear subject, describe your setting and lighting, then choose a visual style. Compare a few prompts and refine what matters instead of stacking too many effects at once.

Polish a photo in three steps

Crop for the right composition, adjust exposure and color, then export at the size you need. Keep a copy of your original image so you can experiment freely.
