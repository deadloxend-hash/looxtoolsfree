Privacy Policy

How LOOX handles local files, cloud processing, preferences and technical data.

Privacy at a glance

Most LOOX tools process files locally in your browser. A tool marked as cloud-powered transfers only the selected content needed for the requested operation after you start processing.

1. Scope and consent

This policy explains data handling across LOOX pages, browser-local utilities and connected processing features. By using the service, you acknowledge the practices described here.

2. How files are processed

Browser-local tools

Text utilities, many converters, image editors, croppers and compressors use browser technologies. Selected files remain on your device and are not sent to a LOOX processing server by those tools.

Connected cloud tools

Some AI features require remote compute. When you start one of these operations, the selected content is transmitted through an encrypted connection to the connected processing service. LOOX does not intentionally archive completed media after returning the result. A connected provider may retain limited operational data under its own terms, so sensitive or confidential media should not be submitted unless necessary.

3. Local preferences

The site may use local or session storage to remember theme, language, sidebar state, favorites, recent tools and tool defaults. These preferences remain on your device and can be cleared through Cookie Settings or browser controls.

4. Technical logs

Hosting and network infrastructure may record standard request data such as an IP address, browser type, timestamps, requested pages and security events. This information supports delivery, reliability, abuse prevention and troubleshooting.

5. Cookies, analytics and advertising

LOOX may use essential storage for preferences. If analytics or advertising services are enabled, their notices and consent choices will be presented as required. LOOX does not sell uploaded media or personal information.

6. Data rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection concerning personal information held by the operator. Because most local-tool files never reach LOOX, the operator cannot retrieve files that remained only on your device.

7. Children

LOOX is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly request personal information from them. A parent or guardian who believes a child provided personal information may contact support.

8. Security and retention

Reasonable technical safeguards are used for platform delivery and encrypted transfers. No internet transmission is risk-free. Operational records are retained only as long as reasonably needed for their stated purpose, legal obligations or security.

9. Policy updates

Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.

10. Contact

For privacy inquiries or data requests, contact our support team at looxservice446@gmail.com.
