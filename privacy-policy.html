Privacy Policy

How LOOX handles local files, cloud processing, preferences and technical data.

Connected accounts, AI and storage — updated 21 November 2026

Supabase processes email authentication and stores account, credit, subscription, generation-job, AI conversation and session records. Cloudflare D1 stores creator profiles and social records. Cloudflare R2 stores newer generated media, uploads and creator artwork; some legacy generated media remains in private Supabase Storage. Public templates and posts you choose to publish are available to other visitors. Private generation access uses authenticated requests or expiring signed links; anyone holding a signed link may use it until expiry.

AI features send the prompt, relevant conversation context and selected uploads to the configured processing service. Depending on the model and availability, processing may use LOOX's GPU worker, Cloudflare Workers AI, Replicate, or configured external AI services. Hosting and delivery involve GitHub Pages, Vercel and Cloudflare. Do not submit sensitive personal information unless you are comfortable with this processing. Local editing tools process files in your browser unless the selected feature explicitly calls a connected service.

Records and media do not all have a verified automatic deletion schedule. Account and generation records may remain until deletion is requested and processed; security, financial and backup records may need separate retention. Contact support for a complete data export, deletion request, or a provider-specific retention question. A complete export of all Cloudflare and Supabase data is not yet available as a self-service download. Account deletion in Settings verifies your password, but cross-service media cleanup is still under review; contact support to confirm complete removal.

Authentication tokens are stored by the Supabase client in localStorage when Remember me is selected, or sessionStorage otherwise. Account suggestions contain email addresses, not passwords, and can be forgotten in Switch account. Workspace preferences, draft prompts and user-specific session selections may also be saved in this browser. Use a trusted device and sign out on shared devices.

There are no configured analytics or marketing trackers in this release. Cookie Settings lets you save or withdraw optional preferences. External fonts, libraries, embedded or remotely loaded media and infrastructure logs can still send network request information to their hosts.

Real checkout remains disabled. Payment, refund and cancellation terms must be reviewed before payments are enabled. Privacy rights, age requirements, international transfers and legally required retention depend on jurisdiction and need professional review; this notice does not assert certification or universal legal compliance. Contact looxservice446@gmail.com for requests.

Accounts and AI generation

Supabase manages account credentials. AI image and video requests use the selected model's configured processing route, which may include a local GPU or an enabled cloud fallback. Newer media uses Cloudflare R2 and legacy media can remain in Supabase Storage. Private result links are generally signed for five minutes. Job and prompt records remain associated with account history until their applicable deletion process is completed.

When you use LOOX AI Chat while signed in, your messages and AI replies are stored in your account so you can reopen and continue conversations on other devices. You can delete individual conversations from Recent chats. Conversation data is not shared with other LOOX accounts. AI chat prompts may be sent to the configured model providers to produce replies.

We also record job status, credit changes and billing references. A keyed email fingerprint is retained after deletion to prevent a second signup bonus. The checkout provider handles payment details and may retain receipts. Use Account Settings for sign-in account deletion and contact support to verify cleanup of associated media and Cloudflare creator/social data.

Privacy at a glance

Most LOOX tools process files locally in your browser. A tool marked as cloud-powered transfers only the selected content needed for the requested operation after you start processing.

1. Scope and consent

This policy explains data handling across LOOX pages, browser-local utilities and connected processing features. By using the service, you acknowledge the practices described here.

2. How files are processed

Browser-local tools

Text utilities, many converters, image editors, croppers and compressors use browser technologies. Selected files remain on your device and are not sent to a LOOX processing server by those tools.

AI generation and connected processing

AI generation and chat require processing outside the user's browser. Data is encrypted in transit between the LOOX website, its backend, storage and configured AI services. The generation service is managed by LOOX. Saved output files and account conversations are retained as described above.

3. Local preferences

The site may use local or session storage to remember theme, language, sidebar state, favorites, recent tools and tool defaults. These preferences remain on your device and can be cleared through Cookie Settings or browser controls.

4. Technical logs

Hosting and network infrastructure may record standard request data such as an IP address, browser type, timestamps, requested pages and security events. This information supports delivery, reliability, abuse prevention and troubleshooting.

5. Cookies, analytics and advertising

LOOX may use essential storage for preferences. If analytics or advertising services are enabled, their notices and consent choices will be presented as required. LOOX does not sell uploaded media or personal information.

6. Data rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection concerning personal information held by the operator. Because most local-tool files never reach LOOX, the operator cannot retrieve files that remained only on your device.

7. Children

LOOX is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly request personal information from them. A parent or guardian who believes a child provided personal information may contact support.

8. Security and retention

Reasonable technical safeguards are used for platform delivery and encrypted transfers. No internet transmission is risk-free. Operational records are retained only as long as reasonably needed for their stated purpose, legal obligations or security.

9. Policy updates

Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.

10. Contact

For privacy inquiries or data requests, contact our support team at looxservice446@gmail.com.
