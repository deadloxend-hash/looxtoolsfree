How to use LOOX Photo Studio

Open a JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF or AVIF photo to start editing locally in your browser. The guide appears before upload; the editor fills the screen once a photo is loaded.

Open, crop and resize

Use Open image to replace the current photo. Tools rotates or flips the image. Use the dedicated Crop button in the bottom toolbar on mobile, or in the left toolbar on desktop, to crop the main image while preserving the canvas size and other layers. Tools still offers Crop canvas for trimming the full composition. Both use eight handles, ratios and precise pixel fields. When a photo is larger than the canvas, Photo Crop starts at the visible canvas boundaries. Resize offers social sizes and custom width and height, with Fit or Fill.

Adjust and add filters

Click a photo on the canvas or choose it in Layers. Adjust changes its light and color. Filters opens a compact bottom panel with Natural, Cool, Warm, Vivid, Blur, Noise, Grain, Vintage and B&W looks. Preview a look, tap ✓ to apply, or × to cancel. Original removes a filter without deleting manual adjustments. The background can be selected separately.

Retouch and compose

Retouch paints or erases a blur mask on the selected photo. Elements adds shapes, stickers and photo overlays. Layers lets you choose, hide, lock, reorder or delete each overlay. Tap a text, heading or caption to type directly. Hold and drag it to move smoothly; when selected, drag the visible corner or edge handles to change the text width and height. Text provides font size, outline, shadow and position; Frames adds borders.

Export a separate copy

Select PNG, JPEG or WebP, image quality where supported, and original, 320, 720, 1080, 2K or 4K resolution. The dialog calculates the actual encoded file size before download. Check the saved photo before sharing it.

On phones, swipe the bottom toolbar sideways to reach more tools. Tap a photo to select it. Drag with one finger to move it, or resize it with the purple edge and corner handles. Use two fingers to resize the photo: spread fingers apart to enlarge it (even beyond canvas edges), or bring them together to shrink it. When the picture is close to filling the canvas, it snaps neatly to the canvas edges in either direction. The canvas dimensions stay unchanged. Tap empty canvas to hide the handles. Tap a tool to open its controls while the preview stays in the editor. Crop mode shows a small cancel cross to the left of the canvas and an apply checkmark to the right. The area outside the canvas is black; transparency remains inside the canvas. Preset backgrounds are separate from the photo and can be recolored live in Resize. Sliders adjust when you drag their thumb, not when you tap the track. Your source photo is not overwritten.
