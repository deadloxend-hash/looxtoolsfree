LOCAL / NETWORK

Your files deserve a clear explanation

The blur operation runs locally in your browser. Your selected photo is not uploaded to LOOX by this tool. If the image is highly sensitive, remember that the original file remains on your device and may still exist in browser memory until the page is closed.

A thoughtful final check

Review identifying details, confidential information and the final file before sharing. Local processing does not automatically remove metadata or guarantee anonymity. AI-generated or heavily edited content may need clear labeling.

LOOX HELP

Questions, answered

Can it automatically detect faces?

Yes. Tap AI Blur All Faces to detect and blur faces in one click using MediaPipe, with face-api.js as a backup. You can still use the manual brush.

Can I blur more than one face?

Yes. Paint over each face or identifying area in the image.

Does the photo leave my device?

The blur workflow is local in your browser and does not upload the photo to LOOX.

Will this replace my original file?

The tools create a separate output. Keep the original until you have checked the exported file; the site does not provide cloud project backups.

Can I publish the result?

Use source media you own or have permission to edit. Check the destination’s requirements and the rights of anyone shown before publishing.
