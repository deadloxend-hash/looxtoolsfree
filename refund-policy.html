Refund Policy

Refunds, cancellations and payment support. Updated November 24, 2026.

Payment availability

Real checkout is currently disabled. This policy describes how future paid LOOX AI purchases will be handled. Sandbox transactions are simulations and do not involve real money.

Refund requests

For purchases processed by Paddle once live payments are enabled, refund requests are handled under Paddle's buyer terms and applicable consumer law. Requests involving duplicate charges, unauthorized payments, technical failures or undelivered purchases may be submitted for review. Nothing in this policy limits mandatory consumer rights, including applicable withdrawal rights.

Subscriptions and credits

Cancel a recurring subscription before its next renewal to avoid future charges. Cancellation does not automatically refund earlier payments. One-time credit purchases do not renew. Refund eligibility for used or unused credits is assessed under applicable law and the payment provider's terms. Failed AI jobs are subject to the credit release rules in our Terms of Use.

How to request a refund

Contact looxservice446@gmail.com or, for Paddle purchases, Paddle buyer support. Include your purchase email, order reference and reason for the request. Never send full card details or passwords. Approved refunds are generally returned through the original payment method; processing time depends on the provider and bank.
