# LOOX original tool artwork

The tool illustrations were generated for LOOX with the built-in image generator. The icons and numbered workflow overlays are original SVG source created for this project. Competitor screenshots were used only as layout references and are not published.

These are concept illustrations, not screenshots or measured before-and-after results. No performance reduction or output-quality claim should be inferred from the artwork.

Original prompts are retained in content/generated-assets.json. Optimized delivery images live in assets/tool-art, icons in assets/icons, and guide content in assets/guides. These materials do not grant rights to third-party logos, photographs or user uploads, and do not guarantee exclusivity, search ranking or advertising approval.
