A useful GIF makes its point quickly. A short demonstration, a reaction or a repeating movement usually works better than a long scene. Start with the smallest section that tells the story and let the loop do the rest.

LOOX TOOLS

Keep the loop light

More frames and a wider image increase the file size. Pick a modest width and frame rate, then check whether the motion still reads clearly. GIF has limited color and no audio; a normal video can be a better choice when smooth motion and small files matter most.

  • Make short looping animations from video.
  • Control output width and frame rate.
  • Useful for quick previews and lightweight reactions.

GIF files can become very large. Trim your clip first, choose a modest width and use a frame rate that is smooth enough for the motion. Consider MP4 or WebM when you do not specifically need GIF.

LOCAL / NETWORK

Your files deserve a clear explanation

Conversion runs locally in your browser with a WebAssembly media engine. Your video is not uploaded to LOOX by the conversion workflow. The engine is downloaded from a CDN, and conversion can use a lot of memory.

A thoughtful final check

Review identifying details, confidential information and the final file before sharing. Local processing does not automatically remove metadata or guarantee anonymity. AI-generated or heavily edited content may need clear labeling.

LOOX HELP

Questions, answered

Why is my GIF file large?

GIF stores animation frames with a limited color palette. Longer clips, larger dimensions and higher frame rates increase file size.

Can I use a long video?

Short clips are recommended. Long videos can take longer and create very large files.

Are videos uploaded?

The conversion is processed locally in your browser; the media engine is loaded from a CDN.

Will this replace my original file?

The tools create a separate output. Keep the original until you have checked the exported file; the site does not provide cloud project backups.

Can I publish the result?

Use source media you own or have permission to edit. Check the destination’s requirements and the rights of anyone shown before publishing.
