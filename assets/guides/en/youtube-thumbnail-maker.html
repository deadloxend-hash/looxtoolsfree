LOOX TOOLS

Think small before you publish

Build on the 1280 × 720 canvas and check the design at a much smaller scale. Move text away from busy details and keep important elements inside safe margins. Use your own artwork or properly licensed material, and avoid misleading claims just to attract a click.

  • Use a standard 16:9 thumbnail canvas.
  • Edit text layers and visual layout.
  • Create an export without leaving the browser.

Make the subject and headline readable at a small size. Use strong contrast, keep text short and avoid placing important details close to the edges where platform previews may crop them.

LOCAL / NETWORK

Your files deserve a clear explanation

The editor works locally in your browser. Images you choose are loaded into the page for editing and are not uploaded to LOOX by the editor. Keep private source images on trusted devices and review any external artwork you choose to add.

A thoughtful final check

Review identifying details, confidential information and the final file before sharing. Local processing does not automatically remove metadata or guarantee anonymity. AI-generated or heavily edited content may need clear labeling.

LOOX HELP

Questions, answered

What size is the canvas?

The editor starts with a 1280 × 720 pixel, 16:9 thumbnail canvas.

Can I use my own background?

Yes. Use the upload background control in the editor.

Are my designs saved to an account?

The editor runs in your browser and exports a file. Download your work to keep a copy.

Will this replace my original file?

The tools create a separate output. Keep the original until you have checked the exported file; the site does not provide cloud project backups.

Can I publish the result?

Use source media you own or have permission to edit. Check the destination’s requirements and the rights of anyone shown before publishing.
