LOCAL / NETWORK

Your files deserve a clear explanation

This utility processes text and generates results locally in your browser. Input, generated passwords and outputs are not uploaded to LOOX by the tool. Preferences are stored on this device, but this module does not save drafts or passwords to local storage. Browser extensions, clipboard history and a compromised device can still expose sensitive material.

A thoughtful final check

Review identifying details, confidential information and the final file before sharing. Local processing does not automatically remove metadata or guarantee anonymity. AI-generated or heavily edited content may need clear labeling.
