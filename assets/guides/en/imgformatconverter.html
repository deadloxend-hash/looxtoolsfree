LOOX TOOLS

Know what can change

JPEG does not preserve transparency, and conversion may not retain metadata. Lossy formats can change fine detail even when dimensions stay the same. Export a separate copy, inspect it against the source, and keep the original available for future edits.

  • Switch between common image formats.
  • Prepare images for websites and apps.
  • Keep the original file while checking the converted result.

Choose PNG for sharp graphics or transparency, JPEG for photographic images, and WebP for modern web delivery. Not every format carries the same metadata or transparency support, so verify the converted image after export.

LOOX HELP

Questions, answered

Will conversion keep every detail?

Lossless formats can preserve pixels, while JPEG and some other compressed formats may change image detail. Metadata may not be retained.

Why is a format missing or not exporting?

Browser support varies by format. Try PNG, JPEG or WebP if a less common format cannot be exported.

Is the image uploaded?

Conversion happens in your browser; the selected image is not sent to LOOX by this workflow.

Will this replace my original file?

The tools create a separate output. Keep the original until you have checked the exported file; the site does not provide cloud project backups.

Can I publish the result?

Use source media you own or have permission to edit. Check the destination’s requirements and the rights of anyone shown before publishing.
