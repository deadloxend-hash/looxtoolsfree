LOOX TOOLS

Start with sound you can use

The source video must contain an audio track. Extraction cannot restore missing sound or automatically remove background noise. Listen to the downloaded file before sharing, and make sure you have permission to reuse the recording and any music it contains.

LOCAL / NETWORK

Your files deserve a clear explanation

The conversion workflow runs locally in your browser using a WebAssembly media engine. Your video is not uploaded to LOOX by this tool. The engine is downloaded from a CDN; processing can be memory-intensive.

A thoughtful final check

Review identifying details, confidential information and the final file before sharing. Local processing does not automatically remove metadata or guarantee anonymity. AI-generated or heavily edited content may need clear labeling.

LOOX HELP

Questions, answered

Does it extract all audio tracks?

The output depends on the media engine’s handling of the source video. Check the downloaded MP3 after conversion.

Why is conversion slow?

The browser has to load the media engine and decode the video before encoding audio.

Are my videos uploaded?

The conversion runs locally in your browser; only the media engine is fetched from a CDN.

Will this replace my original file?

The tools create a separate output. Keep the original until you have checked the exported file; the site does not provide cloud project backups.

Can I publish the result?

Use source media you own or have permission to edit. Check the destination’s requirements and the rights of anyone shown before publishing.
