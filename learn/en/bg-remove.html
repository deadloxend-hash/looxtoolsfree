Background Remover

Give your subject a clean stage, from a product listing to your next creative composition.

The edge is where a cutout succeeds. A clean outline makes a product feel ready for its new setting; a rough one makes even a strong photograph feel unfinished. Begin with a sharp, well-lit image and give fine details a little extra attention.

How to use Background Remover

01

LOOX TOOLS

Keep the details that matter

Hair, transparent glass and soft shadows can be challenging for automatic removal. Inspect the preview closely, use the available touch-up controls, and choose an export that supports transparency. Background removal sends the selected image to a connected service, so decide whether the photo is appropriate to upload before you begin.

  • Useful for product photos, profile graphics and simple creative compositions.
  • A transparent result can be placed over a new color, image or layout.
  • The preview lets you review the result before saving it.

Use a well-lit image with a clear subject/background difference. Hair, glass, fine fabric and motion blur can be difficult for automated cutouts, so always inspect the edges and use manual touch-up where available.

This tool uses cloud AI processing. The image you choose is sent to the connected processing service to remove its background. Do not upload confidential or sensitive images unless you are comfortable with that transfer. Review the service provider’s privacy terms before use.

A thoughtful final check

Review identifying details, confidential information and the final file before sharing. Local processing does not automatically remove metadata or guarantee anonymity. AI-generated or heavily edited content may need clear labeling.

LOOX HELP

Questions, answered

Does it work with every image?

Results vary with image quality, subject edges, shadows and background complexity. Some photos need manual touch-up.

Will my result have a transparent background?

The tool is designed to produce a cutout. Confirm the exported file supports transparency and inspect it in an editor before relying on it.

Are my images processed locally?

No. This page uses cloud AI processing, so selected images are sent to the processing service.

Will this replace my original file?

The tools create a separate output. Keep the original until you have checked the exported file; the site does not provide cloud project backups.

Can I publish the result?

Use source media you own or have permission to edit. Check the destination’s requirements and the rights of anyone shown before publishing.
