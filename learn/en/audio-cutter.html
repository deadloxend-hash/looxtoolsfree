LOOX TOOLS

Know the output format

The cutter exports WAV audio. WAV is uncompressed and can be larger than an MP3 even when the selected excerpt is shorter. Work from a recording your browser can decode, choose precise times, and listen to the saved section before sending it on.

  • Make a short clip from a longer recording.
  • Set timings in seconds.
  • Save the selected section as WAV.

WAV is a high-quality uncompressed format, so it can take more storage than an MP3. If file size matters, use a separate audio encoder after cutting.

LOCAL / NETWORK

Your files deserve a clear explanation

Audio decoding and cutting are performed locally in your browser with Web Audio APIs. The selected audio is not uploaded to LOOX. The WAV output is uncompressed and may be larger than the source.

A thoughtful final check

Review identifying details, confidential information and the final file before sharing. Local processing does not automatically remove metadata or guarantee anonymity. AI-generated or heavily edited content may need clear labeling.

LOOX HELP

Questions, answered

Why is the WAV larger than my MP3?

WAV is usually uncompressed, while MP3 uses lossy compression.

Which formats work?

The browser must be able to decode the selected audio format. MP3, WAV, AAC and M4A are common options.

Is audio uploaded?

No. Cutting runs locally in your browser.

Will this replace my original file?

The tools create a separate output. Keep the original until you have checked the exported file; the site does not provide cloud project backups.

Can I publish the result?

Use source media you own or have permission to edit. Check the destination’s requirements and the rights of anyone shown before publishing.
