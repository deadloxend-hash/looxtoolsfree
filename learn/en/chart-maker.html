Start creating a chart ↗

STEP BY STEP

How to make a chart

  1. Choose a chart type. Select Pie, Donut, Bar, Horizontal, or Line. Pie and donut charts are best for parts of a whole; bars compare categories; lines show trends.
  2. Enter your data. Under the Data tab, replace the sample labels and values with your own. The preview updates as you type.
  3. Add more points. Tap Add row for additional categories, or Remove row to delete the last one. Use Reset to return to the example data.
  4. Name your chart. Type a descriptive chart title so readers understand what the numbers mean.
  5. Customize the appearance. Open Colors to edit colors. In Settings, change the legend position and choose whether to display the legend or grid.
  6. Download. Choose the PNG export width and height under Settings, then press Download PNG Chart to save the finished image.
TIPS

Make your chart easier to read

Keep labels short, use consistent units, and avoid too many slices in a pie chart. For comparisons, use bars with a zero baseline. For changes over time, use a line chart with categories in chronological order. Always double-check your values before sharing.

PRIVACY & EXPORT

Where does your data go?

Chart editing and PNG generation run in your browser. The charting library is loaded from a third-party CDN, so an internet connection is needed to load that library. LOOX does not require an account or upload your chart data to its backend for this tool.

Common questions

Can I edit my chart after downloading?

You can change the data while the page remains open and download another PNG. The exported PNG is an image, not an editable chart project.

Why is my chart blank?

Check that your labels are not empty, values are valid numbers, and the chart library loaded successfully. Refresh the page if your connection was interrupted.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. The controls stack on smaller screens, and the data table can scroll horizontally without moving the whole page.
