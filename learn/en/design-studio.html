LOOX TOOLS

Design for the place it will appear

Choose the canvas size first. A portrait story and a wide banner need different arrangements, even when they share the same message. Keep important details away from the edges and use a small, consistent palette. Review the final image at the size your audience will actually see.

  • Start with common social and creator canvas sizes.
  • Add text, shapes and your own images.
  • Export a PNG directly from the editor.

Give the main headline the strongest visual priority. Use a limited color palette, leave comfortable margins and preview the graphic at the size where people will see it.

LOCAL / NETWORK

Your files deserve a clear explanation

The design canvas is handled in your browser. Images you upload are drawn into the local canvas and are not uploaded to LOOX by this editor. Download your work to keep a copy; this lightweight editor does not provide cloud project storage.

A thoughtful final check

Review identifying details, confidential information and the final file before sharing. Local processing does not automatically remove metadata or guarantee anonymity. AI-generated or heavily edited content may need clear labeling.

LOOX HELP

Questions, answered

Can I create a story or thumbnail?

Yes. Use the Story 9:16 or YouTube 16:9 canvas preset.

Can I move elements?

Select an element and use the position controls to adjust its placement.

Where is my project saved?

Export the design as a PNG to keep a local copy.

Will this replace my original file?

The tools create a separate output. Keep the original until you have checked the exported file; the site does not provide cloud project backups.

Can I publish the result?

Use source media you own or have permission to edit. Check the destination’s requirements and the rights of anyone shown before publishing.
