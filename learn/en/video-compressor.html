LOOX FIELD NOTES

Video Compressor

Make a video easier to send, upload and keep—while protecting the detail your audience needs.

A large video is not always a better viewing experience. If a clip is destined for a phone screen or an email attachment, a thoughtful reduction in resolution and bitrate can make it much easier to share. Choose settings around how people will watch, not just the smallest possible number.

LOOX TOOLS

A sensible size starts with the source

Fast movement, tiny text and noisy footage need more data to look clean. Begin with balanced compression, lower the output width only as far as the destination allows, and check a busy section of the result. Encoding takes time and memory; a smaller output is not guaranteed for every source.

  • Reduce file size for easier sharing.
  • Choose between smaller, balanced and higher-quality output.
  • Keep the processing controls in one simple workspace.

A lower output width and stronger compression usually reduce file size but can soften details. Keep the original file and compare the result on the screen where it will be watched.

LOCAL / NETWORK

Your files deserve a clear explanation

Compression runs locally in your browser using a WebAssembly media engine. Your video is not uploaded to LOOX by the compression workflow. The processing engine is downloaded from a CDN; large videos can use substantial device memory.

A thoughtful final check

Review identifying details, confidential information and the final file before sharing. Local processing does not automatically remove metadata or guarantee anonymity. AI-generated or heavily edited content may need clear labeling.

LOOX HELP

Questions, answered

Why does compression take a while?

Video encoding is resource-intensive and depends on the video length, resolution and your device.

Does the video leave my device?

The compression workflow runs locally in your browser. The engine itself is downloaded from a CDN.

What output format does it create?

The current tool is configured to export MP4.

Will this replace my original file?

The tools create a separate output. Keep the original until you have checked the exported file; the site does not provide cloud project backups.

Can I publish the result?

Use source media you own or have permission to edit. Check the destination’s requirements and the rights of anyone shown before publishing.
