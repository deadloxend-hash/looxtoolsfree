About LOOX

LOOX is an independent software project dedicated to building fast, accessible utilities for creators, developers and everyday users. Our mission is to simplify media editing, file conversion and text processing without mandatory downloads, forced registration or needlessly complicated workflows.

Our technology and privacy commitment

  • Browser-first utilities: Most text, image and document tools process files locally with browser technologies. Those files remain on your device.
  • Connected cloud features: Operations that require remote AI processing clearly identify the transfer before you start and use encrypted connections.
  • Clear product status: Planned features are described as concepts until they are available.

An expanding creative suite

LOOX brings precision resizing, compression, conversion, text transformation, canvas editing and media workflows into one consistent workspace. We publish practical guides and plain-language processing notes so people can make informed decisions about their files.
