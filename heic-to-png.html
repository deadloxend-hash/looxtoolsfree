LOOX FIELD NOTES

HEIC to PNG

Convert your HEIC source into a separate downloadable image.

Formats solve different problems. JPEG is practical for photographs, PNG preserves transparency and crisp graphics, and WebP offers another option for web delivery. A conversion creates a new representation of the same source; it does not recover lost JPEG detail or make a small image genuinely high resolution. Keep an original when transparency, camera metadata or a multi-page document matters.

LOOX HELP

Questions, answered

What does HEIC to PNG do?

Convert your HEIC source into a separate downloadable image.

Does it change my original file?

No. The workspace exports a separate result. Keep the source if you may want another version.

What should I check before using the result?

Browser encoders produce the selected output type. HEIC and TIFF use downloadable decoders; AVIF support depends on the browser. Animated and multi-image sources are represented by a still frame unless a page selection is provided. PDF pages are rendered individually. Export may omit source metadata, so retain original files when provenance matters.

Are there any practical limits?

Very large files can exceed browser memory. Specialized source formats need a supported decoder, and the saved image may not retain source metadata.

Will this replace my original file?

The tools create a separate output. Keep the original until you have checked the exported file; the site does not provide cloud project backups.

Can I publish the result?

Use source media you own or have permission to edit. Check the destination’s requirements and the rights of anyone shown before publishing.
