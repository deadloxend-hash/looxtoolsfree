Cookie Policy

How LOOX uses local storage, session storage and optional cookies.

Browser storage inventory — 21 November 2026

Essential storage includes the Supabase authentication session (loox-account-v1), persistence choice (loox-auth-storage), authentication recovery state and privacy preferences (loox-consent-v1). Authentication storage is managed by the account SDK; passwords are not saved by LOOX. Remember me uses persistent localStorage; otherwise the session uses sessionStorage.

Requested workspace settings include theme, language, layout, favorite and recent tools. Draft prompts and user-specific pinned or active creation sessions can also use browser storage. Switch account may remember up to five email suggestions; choose Forget account suggestions to remove them.

No analytics or marketing tracking integration is configured in this release. We therefore do not show an automatic consent banner solely for essential storage. Optional categories are Essential, Analytics, Functional and Marketing; essential storage stays enabled. Optional choices are stored on this origin for up to 180 days and can be replaced or withdrawn using Cookie Settings. These choices apply to this browser, not every device or domain. New optional integrations must be declared and gated before loading; browser preferences cannot control unrelated third-party domains or infrastructure logs.

Essential workspace storage

LOOX uses browser storage to remember settings such as theme, language, sidebar layout, favorites, recent tools and export defaults. These values support the interface and do not contain uploaded file contents.

Optional third-party services

Connected analytics, advertising or cloud processing services may set or read cookies when those services are enabled. Where required, controls will be provided before non-essential storage is activated.

Your choices

You can clear LOOX preferences on the Cookie Settings page. You can also manage site data through your browser.
