How LOOX uses local storage, session storage and optional cookies.

Browser storage inventory — 27 November 2026

Essential storage includes the Supabase authentication session (loox-account-v1), persistence choice (loox-auth-storage), authentication recovery state and privacy preferences (loox-consent-v1). Authentication storage is managed by the account SDK; passwords are not saved by LOOX. Remember me uses persistent localStorage; otherwise the session uses sessionStorage.

Small raster profile pictures may be cached in sessionStorage under loox-avatar-image with a five-minute validity period and a 96 KiB image-size limit. They are displayed only after verifying the same account, and other account previews are cleared on account changes or sign-out. Creator-avatar references may also be cached for five minutes. These essential interface caches contain no authentication tokens.

Requested workspace settings include theme, language, layout, favorite and recent tools. Draft prompts and user-specific pinned or active creation sessions can also use browser storage. Switch account may remember up to five email suggestions; choose Forget account suggestions to remove them.

No analytics or marketing tracking integration is configured in this release. We therefore do not show an automatic consent banner solely for essential storage. Optional categories are Essential, Analytics, Functional and Marketing; essential storage stays enabled. Optional choices are stored on this origin for up to 180 days and can be replaced or withdrawn using Cookie Settings. These choices apply to this browser, not every device or domain. New optional integrations must be declared and gated before loading; browser preferences cannot control unrelated third-party domains or infrastructure logs.

Google sign-in and temporary verification storage

When Google sign-in is configured and you choose it, LOOX requests only basic identity permissions (openid, email and profile), not Gmail messages, contacts, Drive, Calendar or Photos. A short-lived sign-in correlation record (loox-oauth-attempt-v1) and PKCE verifier use sessionStorage for this requested sign-in. Requests expire after ten minutes; records are removed when processed or replaced, or when the tab closes. Supabase session credentials remain in the selected authentication storage. LOOX does not persist Google provider access or refresh tokens. Google manages its own sign-in cookies under its policies.

Retention and clearing stored data

Authentication sessions last until sign-out, provider expiry or browser storage clearing. Session storage ends with the browser tab; localStorage has no automatic expiry unless a feature implements one. Theme, language, favorites, recent tools and workspace preferences remain until you reset them or clear site data. Exported files and editor projects you save to your device remain under your control. Where an editor stores a project in IndexedDB, remove it within that editor or clear site data. Clearing storage can remove unsaved work.

The social API uses short-lived rate-limit counters for security. IP-based counters use a daily changing hash of the network address; their cleanup target is two days. These counters do not enable analytics or advertising. Provider infrastructure may retain separate access and security logs under its own settings.

Essential workspace storage

LOOX uses browser storage to remember settings such as theme, language, sidebar layout, favorites, recent tools and export defaults. These values support the interface and do not contain uploaded file contents.

Optional third-party services

Connected analytics, advertising or cloud processing services may set or read cookies when those services are enabled. Where required, controls will be provided before non-essential storage is activated.

Your choices

You can clear LOOX preferences on the Cookie Settings page. You can also manage site data through your browser.
