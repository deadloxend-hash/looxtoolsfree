Cookie Policy

How LOOX uses local storage, session storage and optional cookies.

Essential workspace storage

LOOX uses browser storage to remember settings such as theme, language, sidebar layout, favorites, recent tools and export defaults. These values support the interface and do not contain uploaded file contents.

Optional third-party services

Connected analytics, advertising or cloud processing services may set or read cookies when those services are enabled. Where required, controls will be provided before non-essential storage is activated.

Your choices

You can clear LOOX preferences on the Cookie Settings page. You can also manage site data through your browser.
